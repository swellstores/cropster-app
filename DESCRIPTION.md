Send your coffee products and orders from Swell to Cropster for roast production planning. Each paid order becomes a Cropster production order, and its production state shows on the order in Swell.

Connect a Cropster service account with its Client ID and Client Secret, check which product options hold the package size and grind, and turn on the integration. From then on, products and orders are sent as they change, and the Cropster sync page sends the ones you already have.

- **Coffee products with every variant.** Each variant with a SKU goes to Cropster with its package size and grind style, read from your product options, such as 250g, 12 oz, 1 kg or 2 lb.
- **Production orders from paid orders.** Orders are sent when they're paid, or when they're submitted if you choose, as retail or wholesale orders matched by SKU. Item changes are resent while the order is still open in Cropster.
- **Production status on every order.** Every 30 minutes the app copies each order's state back to Swell: Open, Scheduled, In progress, Roasted or Completed.
- **Send what you already have.** The Cropster sync page in your sidebar sends existing products and orders page by page, and lists any record that wasn't sent with the reason.
- **Send to Cropster at any time.** Send a product or order from its Actions menu, or select up to 50 in the list.
- **Errors where you look for them.** When Cropster rejects a product or order, the reason shows on its Cropster tab.

Setup takes a few minutes once your Cropster account has the API Integration add-on. In Cropster, add a service account with the mrp.product and mrp.order scopes, and enter its Client ID and Client Secret in the app settings. Turn on Enable Integration, then open Cropster sync in the sidebar and sync products, then orders.
